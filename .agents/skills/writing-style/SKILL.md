# Writing Style

Read [WRITING_STYLE.md](references/WRITING_STYLE.md) before working on Daniel's
prose. That file owns the voice guidance and accepted examples; keep updates
there so the skill and the translation pipeline use the same reference.

For openings and excerpts, apply
[Earn the Reader's Attention](references/WRITING_STYLE.md#earn-the-readers-attention).
Establish the reader's reason to care through the author's observation before
introducing an unfamiliar project's machinery or promises.

Use the [editorial skill](../editorial-line/SKILL.md) for the governing
argument, headings, depth, and post-versus-series decisions. Use the
[prose skill](../prose-style/SKILL.md) to express the thought in sentences and
paragraphs. Apply these together rather than treating technical precision,
information hierarchy, and an individual voice as competing goals.

Distinguish feedback on content from feedback on expression. When the
material is sound but reads mechanically, preserve the argument and repair
the relationships, rhythm, and emphasis of the prose. Do not compensate by
adding facts, a new thesis, ornate language, or unrequested anecdotes.

Use accepted passages to understand what the author values, not as templates
for new wording. Preserve the author's edits and intended stance within the
requested revision scope. Read the result continuously and judge whether
the reasoning sounds like this author speaking to an intelligent reader;
mechanical checks alone cannot answer that question.
