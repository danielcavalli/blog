# Prose Style

Use this skill when writing or editing sentences and paragraphs, including
when the right content reads like a flat account of successive actions.

Establish the piece's purpose, audience, scope, and governing thought first.
If those are sound, keep them while repairing expression. Criticism of the
writing is not by itself a request for a new outline, different content, or
additional technical detail.

If a blog opening offers only intentions or solution vocabulary, establish
what the reader needs to understand using the
[editorial opening review](../editorial-line/references/blog-composition.md#earn-the-opening).
Shortening such a passage does not repair its missing substance.

Before editing prose, read [the prose contract](references/prose-contract.md)
completely.

Use the author's voice reference when available. For Daniel's blog, use the
[writing-style skill](../writing-style/SKILL.md) alongside this contract.

## Reconstruct thought, not a list of sentences

Before drafting or revising a paragraph, reduce its intended meaning to the
distinct assertions the reader needs and the relationships among them. Decide
which thought leads, what supports it, and what the reader can infer. Rewrite
from that understanding rather than turning each assertion into a separate
sentence. Preserve qualifications that change meaning, scope, stance, or
behavior; remove ones that merely pad the prose.

## Editing sequence

1. List the distinct assertions each paragraph must carry.
2. Identify their relationships and relative importance; remove what the
   purpose and audience do not require.
3. Reconstruct the paragraph so its syntax carries those relationships.
4. Make the governing point easy to find without making every paragraph open
   with the same kind of topic sentence.
5. Read the passage continuously for rhythm, emphasis, unnecessary
   explanation, and the author's voice.
6. Check precision, references, and consistency after the prose works as a whole.

## Required tests

After editing, verify:

- A blog opening communicates a substantive observation and why it matters
  to the subject. Its claims have intelligible referents and supporting context.
- A reader scanning paragraph openings can follow the argument; a reader of
  the complete passage can feel how one thought leads to the next.
- Each paragraph develops one thought; sharing a broad subject is not enough
  to make several independent arguments belong together.
- Every sentence earns its place through substance, necessary orientation,
  or an effect that matters to the meaning.
- Syntax gives the claims appropriate weight instead of reporting every fact
  with the same rhythm and emphasis.
- Removing a qualification would change meaning, stance, scope, or behavior.
- Compression has not erased connective prose, humor, or the author's judgment.
- Domain terms remain consistent and distinct.
- No invented anecdote or new claim was added merely to make the prose lively.

Judge the passage as writing. Sentence counts, added headings, correct facts,
and successful rendering do not establish prose quality. If a blog reader
still has to invent the significance of the opening, revise it before
reporting the prose work complete.
